# ReaxFF charge step supplied by samQEq

The charge equilibration of ReaxFF is a tapered QEq on the cube-root kernel. Here the LAMMPS ReaxFF pair
style runs with its own charge step disabled (`checkqeq no`) and `fix qeq/sam` supplies the charges, with
ReaxFF's electronegativities, hardnesses and shielding parameters under the same taper and one global
neutrality constraint. Two systems: RDX (21 atoms, the force field and geometry of LAMMPS
`examples/reaxff/RDX`, Strachan et al. 2003) and the ReaxFF QEq water (3000 atoms, LAMMPS
`examples/reaxff/water`, Achtyl et al. 2015). Both inputs are copied from the LAMMPS distribution.

Requires a LAMMPS build with `-D PKG_REAXFF=on` in addition to the packages the samQEq build needs.

```bash
for s in rdx water; do
  lmp -in in.${s}_reference -log log.${s}_reference
  lmp -in in.${s}_samqeq -log log.${s}_samqeq
  python3 compare.py $s
done
```

`reference_output/` holds the logs, dumps and `compare.py` output of the runs reported in the paper.
The parameter files are regenerated by `python3 make_params.py`; its docstring gives the two conversions
the agreement depends on: ReaxFF doubles its tabulated hardness, and its Coulomb constant is 14.4 eV Å
rather than the exact value.
